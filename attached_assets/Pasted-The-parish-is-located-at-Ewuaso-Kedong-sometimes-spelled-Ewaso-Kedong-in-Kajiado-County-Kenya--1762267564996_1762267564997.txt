📌 Mission & Community Role

The parish is deeply embedded in community service. For example, it has links with the Ewuaso Osiligi Rescue Center (a girls’ rescue centre nearby) which works to protect girls at risk from FGM and early marriage. The parish is listed as a partner.

The parish is also involved in initiatives such as schooling, using faith‐based education to serve the local Maasai communities. In the article from 2008, it was noted that parents appreciated the school at the parish.

Water and infrastructure projects: The parish-based community in Ewuaso Kedong has benefited from a water project (pipeline from a mountain spring) that serves the broader valley region.

🧩 Notable Features & Challenges

Large geographic coverage: Because of the remote setting and dispersed Maasai communities, the parish covers a wide territory with many smaller out-stations. 
U.S. Catholic
+1

Cultural integration: The parish serves a mainly Maasai population with pastoralist traditions. Engaging local language, culture and remote geography are part of its mission.

Resource needs: While the parish has grown and offers services, the remote locale means infrastructure and access remain challenging (in terms of roads, utilities, outreach).

Community transformation: Through the parish and affiliated centres, there is emphasis on education, especially for girls, and alternative livelihood support (e.g., irrigation, water projects).
